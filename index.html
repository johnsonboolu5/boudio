About the author

Chinua Achebe (1930 to 2013) was a Nigerian novelist, poet, and critic, widely regarded as a central figure in modern African literature. Things Fall Apart has been translated into more than fifty languages and remains one of the most studied novels in the world. It is Book 1 of The African Trilogy, followed by No Longer at Ease and Arrow of God.

Publisher description

Things Fall Apart tells the story of Okonkwo, a proud Igbo leader whose life is ruled by a fear of failure and of resembling his father. Through wrestling fame, family strain, exile, and return, Achebe builds a complete world of proverbs, festivals, justice, and belief, then shows what happens when missionaries and a colonial court arrive and that world begins to fracture from inside and out.
